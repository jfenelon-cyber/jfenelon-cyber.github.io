CLOUD SECURITY CASE STUDY

Cloudflare Zero Trust & Tunnel Deployment

Secure connector deployment and troubleshooting for protected access to an internal camera service.

Cloudflare Zero TrustCloudflare TunnelWindowsDNSRemote Support

Project Objective

Provide secure access to an internal service without exposing unnecessary inbound ports while validating the connector, hostname, route, and local service.

Technical Work

  • Validated tunnel configuration and public hostname mapping.
  • Installed and confirmed the Cloudflare connector on the endpoint.
  • Verified tunnel status and published route.
  • Documented the need to validate the local service address and port.
  • Identified remote-access limitations and provided a professional escalation path.

Outcome

Established the cloud-side tunnel and connector configuration, with remaining local-service validation clearly documented for authorized follow-up.

Portfolio Evidence

This case study summarizes enterprise work while avoiding disclosure of sensitive organizational information.
